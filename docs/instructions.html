CLASSIC MODE

Game Start:

The player selects a game mode (Easy, Normal, Hard, Ultra-Hard).

The game area and target will appear, and the timer will start.

Game Modes Description:

  • Easy: The target appears and stays visible for 2 seconds (2000 ms).
  • Normal: The target appears and stays visible for 1 second (1000 ms).
  • Hard: The target appears for 1 second (1000 ms), with a 50% chance of a fake target appearing, which disappears after 700 ms (clicking it will reset the score).
  • Ultra-Hard: The target appears and disappears in 0.5 seconds (500 ms).

Clicking on the Target:

The player clicks on the moving target.

If they hit it, the score increases, and a sound is played.

If they miss, the score remains the same (or decreases for a fake target).

Time Limit:

The game lasts for 30 seconds.

After the time is up, the game ends and a results window is displayed.

TRACKING MODE

Game Start:

The player clicks the target to begin tracking mode. The ball starts moving right after.

How It Works:

  • Your goal is to follow the moving ball with your mouse for as long as possible.
  • As soon as you lose contact with the ball, the timer stops.
  • The game measures how long you were able to track the ball without losing it.

Scoring:

Your final time is your score — the longer you follow the ball, the better!

SPEED MODE

Game Start:

The player must hold down the button when the light is red, and release it as fast as possible when the green light appears.

How It Works:

  • Red light countdown simulates "Ready...Set..."
  • When the green light turns on, the player must release immediately.
  • Releasing too early shows "False Start" and the player loses.
  • Reaction time is measured in milliseconds (ms).

Scoring:

The faster you release after green, the better your score.

Leaderboard ranks players by best reaction time.
